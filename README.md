## What I'm building

- **VentureQuest** — a climate entrepreneurship simulation where players build and grow a venture through the decisions founders actually face
- **Carbon Markets for SMEs** — an exploration into how AI, better MRV infrastructure, and product design could make carbon-market participation more accessible to smaller businesses in Ghana and, eventually, across Africa
- **[Atmo](https://github.com/davissekai/atmo-v2)** — an AI climate and sustainability assistant built to make complex climate knowledge easier to interrogate, understand, and use
